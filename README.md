# Open Reader

Free, unlimited PDF read-aloud that runs in your browser.

- Shows your real PDF and highlights each sentence as it's read
- Natural voices (Kokoro) that run on your own device, plus your system voices
- Reads maths and physics properly (powers, subscripts, Greek letters, units)
- Installable as an app, works offline, opens PDFs from Finder

Open it: https://krishnpatel-atp.github.io/open-reader/

Your PDFs never leave your computer.
